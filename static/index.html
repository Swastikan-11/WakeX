WakeX Setup Guide
Step 1 of 3
🌐

1. Connect to Local Wi-Fi

WakeX operates strictly local-first on your home network with zero cloud or third-party servers. Ensure your phone and PC are connected to the same Wi-Fi router.

📡 Auto-Discovery: Fast UDP port 8002 discovery active.
⚡ Zero Cloud: Your data never leaves your home network.
🔒

2. Cryptographic Device Pairing

Your phone pairs securely using a one-time 6-digit PIN and stores a 256-bit key in the hardware Android KeyStore. Every power command is HMAC-signed to eliminate replay attacks.

🔑 Find Your PIN: Right-click the WakeX tray icon on your PC and click "Pair Mobile (Show One-Time PIN)".
⚡

3. Instant Wake & Workspaces

You're all set! Use Turn On to wake your PC via Magic Packet, Unlock to wake your monitors and dismiss the lock curtain, or launch automated workspaces.

💡 Hardware Wake Tip: Ethernet wakes from 0W shutdown. Wi-Fi wakes from Modern Standby Sleep while plugged into AC power.

⚠️ Physical Hardware WoL Limits (Honest Reality)
  • Ethernet (Cold Power-Off S5): Works 100% reliably when connected to AC power with motherboard BIOS/UEFI "Wake on LAN / PCIe Devices" enabled.
  • Wi-Fi Wake (WoWLAN): Works while in Sleep or Modern Standby (S0ix) on AC power. True cold 0W power-off wake over Wi-Fi is physically impossible because Wi-Fi radio power drops to 0.
  • Router AP Isolation: In your Wi-Fi router settings, disable "AP Isolation" or "Client Isolation", which prevents phones from reaching PCs on the local network.
